# Schedules

Schedules runs tasks for you at the times you choose. At each run it starts a new conversation in the app you picked, such as Ash, gives it your instructions, and tells you when the run is done. Use it for a morning briefing, a weekly reminder or a check that should happen while you do something else.

## Get started

Open Schedules from the rail and choose **Create**. Give the task a title and the prompt the app is told each time, pick the app it runs in, and choose how often it repeats: every day, on weekdays, every week, every month, once, or a rhythm of your own.

You can also ask for a schedule in a conversation:

> Every Monday at 8:00, suggest three dinners for the week and one shopping list for them.

The list shows each schedule with when it runs next. **Completed** lists the runs, newest first; click one to read its conversation. Schedules also suggests a few tasks that your apps can do: **Add** turns a suggestion into a schedule, and **Dismiss** in its **⋯** menu removes it for good.

## What Schedules asks for

- **Conversations in your other apps.** A run starts a conversation in the app the schedule names, at that app's permission level. A step that needs your yes waits until you answer it.
- **Running in the background.** Schedules keeps running as long as Hearthscale runs, also with its window closed. A run time that passes while your computer sleeps or Hearthscale is closed is skipped.
- **Notifications.** A notification tells you when a run finishes or fails, as each schedule asks.
- **Your chat model.** Schedules asks your chat model for its suggestions.
